# Velo Privacy privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Velo Privacy does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **No network requests.** The extension makes no network requests of its own. Filter
  lists are included in the extension package and are only updated when the extension
  itself is updated.
- **No accounts, analytics or tracking.** There is no sign-in, telemetry, crash
  reporting or remote configuration.
- **Local settings only.** Your settings, site lists, filters and the hash of your
  WhatsApp Web lock password are stored in your browser's local extension storage. They
  never leave your device unless you export them to a file yourself.
- **Browsing data.** To do its job, the extension reads the address of the pages you
  visit, your cookies and your history, and deletes data according to your settings. All
  of this happens locally in your browser.
- **Panic button.** If you turn it on, tabs it hides are saved to a bookmark folder in
  your browser, and the folder is deleted when you restore them.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/algodevX/Velo-Privacy/issues).
